# Analytics and Share Poster Layout Contract

Applies to `我的口味档案`, `最佳饭搭子`, `谁最会种草`, `本月回顾`, and
`年度回顾`, plus their five independent 9:16 posters.

## In-App analysis

1. Compact navigation/title identity.
2. Evidence-based headline and honest sample status.
3. One prominent `生成分享海报` action.
4. A central Food Hero.
5. Surrounding callouts/nodes connected by deliberate lines/anchors.
6. Supporting evidence and representative Restaurant stories with real images.
7. Honest sparse/empty/insufficient states.

The page is a scrollable infographic, not an ordinary dashboard of equal cards.
The Food Hero is data-linked only when the analyzer provides evidence; otherwise
it is explicitly decorative. The five pages share the Analysis visual family
but may vary their narrative sections.

## Required semantics

- Taste Profile: preference rank, spend, scene, source, companion, rating style,
  return rate, keywords, time, consumption, representative Restaurants.
- Best Companion: person name, shared visits, foods, Restaurants, recency,
  relationship pattern, memories, next suggestion.
- Who Recommends Best: usage, average, reliability, `最坑推荐来源`, named
  sources, Restaurant evidence.
- Monthly: visits, Restaurants, new places, spend, favorites, companions,
  sources, taste change, representative Restaurants.
- Annual: yearly totals, new places, spend, favorites, companion, source,
  trajectory, keywords, representative Restaurants.

## Posters

- Independent native 9:16 composition; never a page screenshot.
- Warm/off-white paper, stronger Food/Dining storytelling, central Hero,
  callouts, restraint in receipt/tape details, real Restaurant images when
  available, and one shared `SharePosterBrandFooter`.
- Share/save exists in Poster Preview, not duplicated across the analysis page.
- QR appears only from fail-closed public Release configuration; no fake or
  Development URL.

## States

Rich, sparse, insufficient, empty, long names/text, missing Restaurant photo,
mixed nil ratings/spend, period with no visits, current/previous period,
Light/Dark page, poster rendering/export failure, and physical-device share/save.

## Prohibited

No ordinary KPI-card wall, no fixed same Food Hero regardless of evidence, no
decorative icon presented as a conclusion, no missing real image masked by a
fallback, no third Analysis theme.
